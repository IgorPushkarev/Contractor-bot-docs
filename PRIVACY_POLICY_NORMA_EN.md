## 3. WHAT THE USER RECEIVES

3.1. The result of the Bot's operation is a reference consultation containing:

- An answer to a legal question based on current legislation

- Practical guidance

3.2. The result is reference information. The User may use it at their own discretion, subject to Section 5 of this Policy.

## 4. DATA COLLECTION AND STORAGE

4.1. For the Bot's operation, in particular to enforce the daily request limit and maintain statistics, the Bot **stores a technical minimum of data**:

- **Telegram identifier** (chat_id / user_id) — to send a response and for accounting
- **Telegram username** (if available) — for identification in the administrative panel
- **Date of first request** and **request counter** — to enforce the daily limit (5 requests per day) and prevent abuse
- **Short request history** (technical log: time, duration, query fragment up to 80 characters) — for statistics and improving the Bot

4.2. The Bot **DOES NOT collect or store**:

- Passwords, payment data, identity documents
- Full texts of private correspondence outside the Bot
- Cookies or network tracking identifiers

4.3. **Storage is limited:** the technical request log is kept within the most recent records (with automatic volume limitation) and does not accumulate indefinitely.

4.4. **Data Minimization (GDPR Art. 5(1)(c)):** the Bot processes only the minimum data technically necessary to provide the service and for accounting.
